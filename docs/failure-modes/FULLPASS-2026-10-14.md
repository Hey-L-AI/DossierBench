## Why the old verdicts were replaced

The old 157 KEY_ARTIFACT (19%) mixed 31 fresh reads with 126 verdicts copied from earlier passes. Two
problems:

1. **The copy key ignored the checkpoint.** It matched on (subject, field, element, emitted, gold) but
   not on the onset checkpoint. The same value can be a premature guess at c1 and supported at c5, so
   one verdict was copied onto cases it did not fit.
2. **The earlier passes were lenient.** They contradicted themselves on 37 signatures (found in
   step 4 of the cross-cut analysis's method). Re-read here, the inherited KEY_ARTIFACT verdicts mostly came back
   GENUINE: of those labelled `exact` 56 / 120 and of those labelled `sig` 24 / 34. All 3 old fresh
   KEY_ARTIFACT held.

Example: gen-002 prompted-v2 DeepSeek `event_types_liked` c14 `open_mic` was stored as KEY_ARTIFACT
(`sig`), but its own note describes a fabrication on an unevidenced field.

## Method

1. **Cells, not episodes.** The 827 episodes collapse to **524 distinct cells**: (subject, field,
   constraint atom, emitted, gold, onset checkpoint). 382 cells occur in one run only; the rest are
   shared by 2–9 runs. What the transcript shows at a checkpoint does not depend on which system gave
   the value, so each cell is read once and its verdict applies to every episode in it.
2. **Pass 1 — every cell, blind.** 18 Opus 5.5 sub-agents, about 30 cells each, grouped by subject.
   Each read the whole served transcript before judging. Readers saw no system name and no earlier
   verdict. The rules are the owner rules from `data-crosscut-2026-08-30/scripts/INSTRUCTIONS-gtfix.md`,
   unchanged, so results stay comparable (`data-fullpass-2026-10-14/INSTRUCTIONS.md`).
   Result: 443 GENUINE / 47 KEY_ARTIFACT / 34 AMBIGUOUS cells.
3. **Pass 2 — independent re-read of every non-GENUINE cell.** 6 Opus 5.5 sub-agents re-judged the
   81 KEY_ARTIFACT/AMBIGUOUS cells without seeing pass 1. They also gave a `ka_reason` for each
   KEY_ARTIFACT (`INSTRUCTIONS2-addendum.md`). Pass-1 GENUINE cells were not re-read.
4. **Settling.**
   - Agreement stands.
   - Pass 1 AMBIGUOUS → pass 2 GENUINE is taken as GENUINE (11 cells, 24 episodes). The pass-2
     reasons are all "positive commit before any evidence", which the rules call GENUINE.
   - gen-013 cells 190/193 (allergies = unknown, gold wheat) are GENUINE from run data: the same run
     had no `gluten_free` under dietary at that checkpoint, so wheat was lost, not refiled.
   - The 23 remaining disagreements were decided by the owner on 2026-10-14 (below).

**Reproduce.**
1. Transcripts: `TRANSCRIPTS_OUT=<dir> python3 scripts/dump_transcripts.py` (at the time `runs/dump_all_transcripts.py`; readers used `<dir>/<subject>.md`).
2. Cells and packets: `python3 scripts/build_cells.py <out-dir>` (the data folder's `scripts/`, not shipped) (packet-00 was then split by hand
   into packet-00 / packet-17, since gen-010 has 61 cells).
3. From inside this data directory, run `merge_pass1.py`, `merge_pass2.py` and `finalize.py`. These
   last steps are free and deterministic. The reader passes are not; they are LLM reads.
