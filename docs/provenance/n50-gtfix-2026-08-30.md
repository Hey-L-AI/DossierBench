# n50-gtfix-2026-08-30

**Superseded** (canonical 2026-08-30 to 2026-10-14; see [`CANONICAL.md`](CANONICAL.md)). Supersedes `n50-route1-surfaced-2026-08-30`. Applies the **18 verified gold-defect
fixes** from the 2026-08-30 failure-mode re-analysis (a write-up and worklist in the development

## What changed and why

The re-analysis adjudicated 1,286 SUT-error episodes against the full served transcripts, flagged 37
gold cells, and — after an adversarial verification pass and a pre-commit full-conversation
confirmation (independent Fable-5 sub-agents at each stage) — confirmed **18 defects** across **15
subjects**. All 18 are applied here by a one-off apply script (element-level ops that preserve
every sibling element; `APPLY_CONTESTED=True`). By kind:

- **booking / bot-scenery ≠ liking** (onset move / prune; rules 1.2/1.3): gen-014 `japanese` c6→c15,
  gen-010 `german` c0→c1 + `ramen` c11→c0, gen-031 `caribbean` c3→c12, gen-033 prune `taiwanese` +
  `portuguese` c2→c0, gen-027 prune `japanese`(zero-support) + `portuguese`, gen-013 `allergies`
  c11–14→UNEVIDENCED (bot "no-wheat note"; user first voices wheat c15), gen-047
  `participation_vs_spectating` `watches_only`→`joins_readily` WEAK from c6 (latent contradicted).
- **sibling-vocab** (rule 6.4): gen-040 `japanese`→`sushi`, gen-019 `japanese`→`ramen` (c5–6),
  gen-020 `food_festival`→`market`.
- **user-voiced element gold omits** (add + WEAK from first user surface; never-surfaced latents kept
  in the final card, out of scored `card_at`; rule 7a): gen-017 `event_types_avoided`+=`tasting_menu`
  (c1) & `cuisines_liked`+=`vietnamese` (c5), gen-024 `event_types_avoided`+=`tasting_menu` (c7) +
  `cuisines_liked` burgers c2→c3, gen-027 `event_types_liked` `dance_night` WEAK (c3), gen-030
  `cuisines_liked`+=`spanish` (c3), gen-039 `event_types_liked`+=`dance_night` (c5).
- **latent leaked into a scored cell** (regrade): gen-037 `deal_seeking` WWW→UUU.

## Scope of the diff

Against route1-surfaced: **15 subjects, 18 cells**, confined to their named fields; verified no
collateral. Nine cells changed a **grade** (`provisional`); the rest changed only `card_at` (and the
final `card` where an element was added/pruned/relabeled).

## The two contested reversals (owner adopted A, 2026-08-30)

Two down-regrades reversed an established credit-layer decision and were re-checked with the owner
before applying:

- **gen-013 `allergies` c11–14** reversed a **two-reader key-3 WEAK credit** (both arms). A full sweep
  of c0–c11 confirmed the user says nothing wheat-adjacent before c11; the "no-wheat note" is the bot
  carrying the latent card trait (rule 1.3).
- **gen-024 `cuisines_liked` c2** reversed a **2026-08-25 owner hand-adjudication** that credited
  burgers under the pre-strict rule 1.1; STRICT 1.2 (2026-08-29) supersedes it.

`runs/reconcile_contested_gtfix.py` removed the 3 arm credits (gen-013-s11 allergies ×2 arms,
gen-024-s2 cuisines_liked ×1 arm), dropped the gen-024 adjudication, and filed 2 owner dismissals so
the underlying leaks are refused rather than left blocking. **This is the first base to edit the
credit arms** — hence the `-gtfix` arm variants here, rather than the inherited `-gradefix` arms.

## Reader / verdict re-key

Unlike route1-surfaced (which changed only `card_at`, invisible to the reader prompt), gtfix changes
the **final card** on 7 subjects, which `audit_prompt` shows the reader. So the verdict cache (110
verdicts, `runs/rekey_cache_gtfix.py`) and the dismissals + adjudications
(`runs/rekey_decisions_gtfix.py`) were re-stamped to the new fingerprints — inert (card-line diffs
only; 0 refused). The frozen reading otherwise reused verbatim.
